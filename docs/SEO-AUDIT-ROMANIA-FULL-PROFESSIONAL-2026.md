## 4. AEO – FAQ Questions (40–60 words)

1. **Study in Romania for international students?** 73+ universities, 500+ English programmes. Tuition €400–€9,000; living €300–€600. MBBS €5k–12k; pilot €55–70k. Work 20 hrs/week. Visa Kathmandu or New Delhi.
2. **Romania student visa process for Nepali students?** Honorary Consulate Kathmandu or Embassy New Delhi. Admission, tuition proof, €2,000+, passport 6+ months. €120 fee. 6–8 weeks before travel.
3. **Tuition fees in Romania for Nepali students 2026?** €400–€9,000/year; MBBS €5k–12k. Cheapest: Târgu Jiu, Bacău, Sibiu. 1 EUR ≈ 143 NPR.
4. **Living cost in Romania for students from Nepal?** Bucharest €400–€600; Cluj €350–€550. Dorm €60–€85. Total ~€600/mo.
5. **Study in Romania without IELTS?** Yes. Many accept 12+ years English-medium, TOEFL, Cambridge, or university test.
6. **MBBS in Romania for Nepali students?** 6-year programme. Carol Davila, Iuliu Hațieganu. €5k–12k/year. WHO, ECFMG.
7. **Study pilot in Romania from Nepal?** EASA ATPL €55–70k. Romanian Aviation Academy, Aerostar Bacau. English. EU-valid.
8. **Cheapest universities in Romania?** Constantin Brâncuși, Vasile Alecsandri, Lucian Blaga. €1,400–€4,300/year.
9. **Post-study pathway in Romania?** No dedicated permit. Employment residence via work contract. EU Blue Card for qualified.
10. **Can I get PR in Romania after studying?** PR: 5+ years legal residence. Employment residence → long-term → citizenship. igi.mai.gov.ro.
