## 5. AEO (Answer Engine Optimization)

### 10 FAQ Questions (Schema-Ready, 40–60 Words)

1. **What is study in Georgia from Nepal?**  
   Study in Georgia from Nepal means pursuing undergraduate or postgraduate degrees at Georgian universities. Tuition $2,500–$7,000/year, living $300–500/month. No IELTS at most universities. D3 visa via India. WHO/UNESCO-recognised degrees. Popular for MBBS, business, IT.

2. **How much does it cost to study in Georgia from Nepal?**  
   Total first-year cost $8,000–$15,000 (NPR 10.6–20L): tuition $2,500–7,000, living $3,600–6,000, visa $50–100, airfare ~$600. Medicine $5,000–8,000/year. 1 USD ≈ 133 NPR (2026).

3. **Is IELTS required to study in Georgia from Nepal?**  
   No. Most Georgian universities do not require IELTS or PTE. English-taught programmes assess via interview or prior education. Check each programme.

4. **What is the Georgia student visa process for Nepali students?**  
   Apply for D3 immigration visa at Georgian Embassy/Consulate in India or VFS (no embassy in Nepal). Required: admission letter, funds, insurance, accommodation, passport. MFA decides within 30 days. Register at psh.gov.ge within first 45 days of visa validity.

5. **Where do Nepali students apply for Georgia visa?**  
   No Georgian embassy in Nepal. Apply at Georgian Embassy New Delhi or VFS Global Application Centers in India. Consultancies assist with documents.

6. **Can I work while studying in Georgia?**  
   Yes, up to 20 hours/week during term (verify at psh.gov.ge). Full-time during breaks. Post-study: 1-year permit to seek employment.

7. **What are the top universities in Georgia for Nepali students?**  
   Tbilisi State University (TSU), Ilia State, Georgian Technical University, Tbilisi State Medical University, University of Georgia, Batumi Shota Rustaveli. TSU from 2,250 GEL/year.

8. **Is MBBS from Georgia valid in Nepal?**  
   WHO-listed Georgian medical degrees can be recognised by NMC Nepal. Graduates must pass NMC screening and register. Verify university on NMC list before applying.

9. **What is the Georgia intake deadline 2026?**  
   September main intake—apply May–August. February at some universities. Apply 2+ months before start for visa. Each university sets deadlines.

10. **How long is the Georgia student visa processing time?**  
    MFA decides within 30 calendar days. Apply at least 2 months before semester. Total timeline ~3 months from application to departure.
