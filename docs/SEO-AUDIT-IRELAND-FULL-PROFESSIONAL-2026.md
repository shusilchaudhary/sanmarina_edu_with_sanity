## 3. AEO – 10 FAQ Questions (40–60 words)

1. **What are study in Ireland from Nepal requirements?** Offer letter, passport (6+ months), proof of funds €10,000+ (6 months), English test (IELTS 5.5+ or Duolingo 75), private medical insurance, police clearance. Apply via VFS; NDVO processes from Nepal.
2. **Ireland student visa process for Nepali students?** Submit via VFS. NDVO (New Delhi) processes Nepal. Required: offer letter, €10,000+ funds, English test, insurance. Apply up to 5 months before course start. Processing 4–8 weeks.
3. **Tuition fees in Ireland for Nepali students 2026?** Undergrad €9,850–€28,000; Master's €10,000–€35,000. Cheapest: IPA €3,200; St Patrick's €5,779. 1 EUR ≈ 143 NPR.
4. **Living cost in Ireland for students from Nepal?** Dublin €1,500–€2,000/mo; Cork €1,200–€1,500; Galway €1,200–€1,500. Shared room €600–€1,200. Irish Immigration recommends €10,000/year.
5. **Can I work while studying in Ireland?** Stamp 2: 20 hrs/week term; 40 hrs Christmas & Summer. IRP and PPS required. Stamp 2A (language) cannot work.
6. **What is Stamp 1G?** Third Level Graduate Scheme. Master's/PhD: 24 months; Bachelor's: 12 months. Apply within 6 months of results. Work 40 hrs/week.
7. **Scholarship in Ireland for Nepali students 2026?** GOI-IES: 60 awards, €10,000 + tuition waiver. Deadline 12 March 2026. Master's/PhD. See hea.ie.
8. **Study in Ireland without IELTS?** Ireland accepts Duolingo 75, TOEFL 61, PTE 30. IELTS not mandatory but English test compulsory for visa.
9. **Where do Nepali students apply for Ireland visa?** Via VFS. NDVO (New Delhi) processes Nepal applications. No Irish embassy in Kathmandu.
10. **Can I get PR in Ireland after studying?** PR after 5+ years legal residence. Stamp 1G → employment permit → long-term. Verify at irishimmigration.ie.
