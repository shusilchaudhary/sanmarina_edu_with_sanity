## 4. AEO – 10 FAQ Questions (Structured 40–60 words)

1. **How to study in Greece from Nepal?** Apply to Greek universities, get admission, proof of funds, health insurance, accommodation. Apply Type D visa at Greek Embassy New Delhi. Processing 15–45 days. VAC Kathmandu ceases Jan 2026.
2. **What is Greece student visa process for Nepali students?** Type D visa at Greek Embassy New Delhi. Required: admission letter, passport, proof of funds, health insurance €30,000 min, accommodation. 15–45 working days.
3. **What are tuition fees in Greece for Nepali students 2026?** Public €1,500–€10,000; private €6,000–€15,000; MBA €7,250–€9,900. NPR ≈ 143 per EUR. Cheapest: public institutions.
4. **What is living cost in Greece for students from Nepal?** Athens €670–€1,190/mo; Thessaloniki €585–€1,015/mo (cheaper). Dorm €150–€300/mo. Thessaloniki among cheapest cities.
5. **Can Nepali students work in Greece while studying?** Yes. Minimum 15 hrs/week, outside class hours. Social security applies. Post-study: 1-year job-search permit.
6. **Scholarship in Greece for Nepali students 2026?** Hellenic Ministry scholarships exclude Nepal 2025–26. Erasmus+, Erasmus Mundus, university merit available. studyingreece.gr.
7. **Study in Greece without IELTS from Nepal?** Some accept TOEFL, Cambridge, MOI. International Hellenic University offers English-only programmes.
8. **MBA in Greece for Nepali students requirements?** Bachelor's, IELTS 6.0–6.5, work experience varies. AUEB MBA €7,250–€9,900. Deadlines March–July.
9. **Where do Nepali students apply for Greece student visa?** Greek Embassy New Delhi. VAC Kathmandu ceases Jan 2026. Verify with mfa.gr.
10. **Post-study residence pathway in Greece?** 1-year permit to search for employment. Proof €650+/mo, health insurance, degree. Apply 30 days before student permit expires.
