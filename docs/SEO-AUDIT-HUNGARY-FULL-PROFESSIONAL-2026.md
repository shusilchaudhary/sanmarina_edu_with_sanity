## 3. AEO – 10 FAQ Questions (40–60 words)

1. **Reasons to study in Hungary?** 550+ English programmes, tuition €1,700–€10,800, Stipendium Hungaricum, work 30 hrs/week, 9-month post-study, Schengen. Budapest, Debrecen.
2. **Hungary student visa process for Nepali students?** Honorary Consulate Kathmandu (Jhamsikhel) or Embassy New Delhi. Required: admission, passport, €6,000+ funds, insurance. Email appointments. Min 3 weeks.
3. **Tuition fees in Hungary for Nepali students 2026?** Undergrad €1,700–€10,800; Master's €2,500–€10,800. BME engineering €6,500–€7,800. Nursing €1,700–€3,700/sem. Stipendium covers if eligible.
4. **Living cost in Hungary for students from Nepal?** Budapest €500–€700/mo; Debrecen €350–€500. Dorm €90–€350; shared €200–€450. Debrecen cheaper.
5. **Can I work while studying in Hungary?** Yes. 30 hrs/week term; full-time holidays. No separate permit.
6. **Stipendium Hungaricum for Nepali students?** Tuition + accommodation + stipend. Nepal not currently sending partner—check stipendiumhungaricum.hu annually.
7. **Study in Hungary without IELTS?** Yes. Prior English-medium (12+ yrs), TOEFL, Cambridge, Duolingo accepted. Per university.
8. **Where do Nepali students apply for Hungary visa?** Honorary Consulate Kathmandu (Chaudhary Towers, Jhamsikhel) or Embassy New Delhi.
9. **Post-study pathway in Hungary?** 9-month job-search residence. Apply before student permit expires. No job offer initially.
10. **PR in Hungary after studying?** 9-month post-study → employment permit → long-term residence. PR typically 3+ years legal stay. Verify at Hungarian immigration.
